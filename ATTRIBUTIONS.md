# Asset notes

`01-mooni-feast.png`, `02-mooni-pumpkin.png`, and
`03-mooni-garden.png` were supplied by the project maintainer for the Mooni
theme. The supplied art contains Peanuts characters; no source URL or license
metadata was provided with the files. Confirm redistribution rights before
publishing the theme.

`04-omarchy-wordmark.png` uses the official Omarchy wordmark from the
[Omarchy brand page](https://omarchy.org/brand/), recolored with the Mooni
palette and rendered at 3840×2160.
